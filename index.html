What is ChromaView?

A small studio for choosing colors with confidence.

Whether you are designing an app, decorating a room or making a poster, ChromaView helps you go from “I like this color” to a finished, readable palette. Start from the color wheel or a photo, shuffle until it feels right, then test it so your text is easy to read for everyone, including people with color vision differences.

Features

Everything you need, nothing you don’t.

Wheel & image picker

Drag on the color wheel, type a HEX, RGB or HSL value, or tap any pixel in a photo to borrow its color.

Smart palettes

Generate balanced palettes around your base color. Lock the colors you love and shuffle the rest.

Shades & tints

See lighter and darker versions of any color, with full codes and one-tap copy.

Contrast checker

Test every color pair against the WCAG AA and AAA guidelines so your text is always readable.

Color vision preview

See how a palette looks with protanopia, deuteranopia, tritanopia and achromatopsia.

Save, export & undo

Keep favorite palettes on your device, export them as JSON, and undo any change with a tap.

Set up on Android in 3 steps

  1. Download the APK on your phone using the button or QR code above.
  2. Allow the install if Android asks. Open Settings, then Install unknown apps, and allow your browser.
  3. Open ChromaView. Everything is stored on your phone, so it works with no connection.

On iPhone? Open ChromaView in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It works offline too.
